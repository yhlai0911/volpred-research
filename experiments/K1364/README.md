# K1364 - ETF Sampling Arbitrage Proxy And Liquid-Constituent Heterogeneity

## 問題

`research_program.md` 的待辦問題是：

> ETF sampling arbitrage 是否只放大 liquid constituents 的 vol/comovement - 用 SPY/IVV/VTI/IWM/MDY/sector ETF 公開 holdings + yfinance 成分股流動性 proxy，建「ETF basket sampling tilt」與 tracking-error/volume shock，分 liquid/illiquid tercile 檢定 ETF shock 後成分股 RV、market beta、close-to-close comovement 是否異質。

本實驗是可重現的免費資料 pilot。它不能觀察 authorized participants 的真實 creation/redemption basket，也不能直接證明 JFQA paper 的因果機制；它只問：用公開 top-10 holdings 與日頻價量 proxy 時，是否已經看得到「lagged ETF shock 後，高流動性成分股比低流動性成分股反應更強」的跡象。

## 動機與差異化

- 既有 ETF / fragility 題多看 ETF 或 index level，本題改看同一 ETF basket 內的成分股截面異質性。
- JFQA 2026 的 `ETF Sampling and Index Arbitrage` 指出 ETF sampling 會讓 AP arbitrage 對 liquid stocks 的 market-quality effect 更強；本實驗把這個命題降階為日頻 proxy 可否驗出的問題。
- 與 ETF macro-efficiency、ETF heartbeat、generic ETF fragility 不同，本題聚焦 liquid vs illiquid constituents。

## 文獻脈絡

1. Brogaard, Heath, Huang (2025/2026), *ETF Sampling and Index Arbitrage*, JFQA: ETF AP arbitrage effects differ by constituent liquidity; liquid stocks show stronger volatility / comovement / market-quality effects.
2. Ben-David, Franzoni, Moussawi (2018), *Do ETFs Increase Volatility?*, Journal of Finance: ETF ownership and arbitrage can transmit non-fundamental volatility to underlying stocks.
3. Da and Shive (2018), *Exchange Traded Funds and Asset Return Correlations*, European Financial Management: ETF arbitrage can increase constituent return comovement.
4. Brown, Davies, Ringgenberg (2021), *ETF Arbitrage, Non-Fundamental Demand, and Return Predictability*, Review of Finance: creation/redemption activity is a setting for non-fundamental demand shocks.

## 資料

- ETF universe: `SPY`, `IVV`, `VTI`, `IWM`, `MDY`, and 11 SPDR sector ETFs.
- Holdings: yfinance `Ticker(...).funds_data.top_holdings`, top-10 snapshot, cached under `experiments/K1364/data/`.
- Prices: yfinance adjusted OHLCV, `2020-01-01` to `2026-06-24` exclusive.
- Effective sample and exclusions are written to `K1364_results.json`.
- Seed: `42`.

## 方法

For each ETF-date:

1. Use lagged 63-day average log dollar volume to rank available top-10 constituents into high- and low-liquidity terciles.
2. Build ETF shock as the mean of:
   - rolling-z ETF dollar-volume shock;
   - rolling-z absolute tracking-error proxy between ETF return and normalized top-10 holdings basket return.
3. Apply explicit lag:

```python
etf_shock_lag1 = etf_shock_raw.shift(1)
log_adv_lag1 = rolling_mean_log_dollar_volume.shift(1)
```

4. Regress high-minus-low constituent response on `etf_shock_lag1`, ETF fixed effects, lagged absolute ETF return, lagged market RV, and lagged dependent variable.
5. Cluster standard errors by date and Bonferroni-adjust across the three targets.

Targets:

- `rv_spread`: high-minus-low weighted average of constituent close-to-close `r_t^2` rolling z-scores.
- `market_beta_proxy_spread`: high-minus-low weighted average of rolling-z `stock_ret_t * SPY_ret_t`.
- `etf_comovement_spread`: high-minus-low weighted average of rolling-z `stock_ret_t * ETF_ret_t`.

## Success Criteria

The proxy supports the backlog claim only if at least two of the three target regressions have:

- positive coefficient on `etf_shock_lag1`;
- Bonferroni-adjusted `p < 0.05`;
- event diagnostic that is directionally consistent.

Anything weaker is recorded as `WEAK_DIAGNOSTIC` or `NULL_PROXY`, not as evidence that ETF AP arbitrage causally amplifies liquid constituents.

## Limitations

- This is not AP basket data.
- Current top-10 holdings are used as a snapshot; historical holdings drift and survivorship bias remain.
- Tracking error uses top-10 basket proxy, not official NAV.
- Daily data miss intraday arbitrage and spread/depth dynamics.
- A null result here cannot reject the JFQA finding because the measurement is much coarser.
