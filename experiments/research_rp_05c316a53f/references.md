# References

1. Lou, D., Polk, C., & Skouras, S. `A tug of war: Overnight versus intraday expected returns`.
   - https://personal.lse.ac.uk/polk/research/TugOfWar.pdf
   - Prior: anomaly and momentum returns can decompose very differently across overnight and intraday sessions.

2. Ahn, Y., Fan, A. Q., Noh, D., & Park, S. Y. (2024). `Retail Ebb and Flow and the Overnight-Intraday Return Gap`.
   - https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4752520
   - Prior: investor-type flow can drive the overnight-intraday return gap.

3. He, A. (2025). `Decoding Momentum Spillover Effects`, Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis.
   - https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/decoding-momentum-spillover-effects/EB6BE5A096753108881E1514E54035DF
   - Prior: retail/professional trading split helps explain overnight continuation and daytime reversal.

4. Perreten, T., & Wallmeier, M. (2025). `Overnight Returns and the Timing of Trading Volume`.
   - https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5004991
   - Prior: intraday volume timing predicts overnight returns and challenges a pure retail-pressure explanation.
