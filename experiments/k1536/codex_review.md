# Codex Review — K1536

## Verdict

**PASS with limitations.** The experiment is a valid proxy diagnostic and the reported conclusion is appropriately conservative: public ETF/ETN proxies do not support the hypothesis that high biodiversity-footprint commodity exposure has generally higher realized volatility; selected event windows are directionally positive for RV/downside but not statistically robust.

## Checks

- Required experiment files are present: `README.md`, `k1536.py`, `k1536_results.json`, figures.
- Data source and effective sample are recorded: yfinance adjusted daily closes, 2018-01-03 to 2026-06-22, 2,128 union trading days.
- Randomness is fixed: event-block bootstrap uses `SEED = 42`.
- Lookahead risk is controlled for this design: pre-window uses only `[-20, -1]`; post-window starts at event trading date; there is no predictive trading strategy or same-day signal-return multiplication.
- Null result is reported as null: event-window RV/downside estimates are positive but bootstrap confidence intervals cross zero.
- The code does not hand-edit results; `k1536_results.json` and figures are generated by `k1536.py`.

## Caveats

- This is not a replication of the Review of Finance commodity-futures biodiversity-footprint sort.
- ETF proxies mix commodity exposure, roll mechanics, fund structure, and in `WOOD`'s case equity-market exposure.
- Six events are too few for strong event-study inference.
- The full-sample negative high-minus-control RV result is partly driven by energy/metals controls having large 2020 and 2025-2026 volatility spikes; it is valid for the selected tradable proxy baskets but should not be generalized to physical commodity biodiversity intensity.

## Required Wording Discipline

Acceptable:

- "In public ETF proxies, high biodiversity-footprint commodity exposure did not show higher realized volatility."
- "Event-window reactions are directionally positive but not statistically robust."

Not acceptable:

- "Biodiversity transition risk is not priced."
- "The Review of Finance commodity-futures result is false."
- "Biodiversity events predict commodity tail risk."
