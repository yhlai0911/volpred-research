# Adrian & Brunnermeier (2016) CoVaR

- **Citation**: Adrian, T., & Brunnermeier, M. K. (2016). CoVaR. *American Economic Review* 106(7), 1705–1741.
- **Used in K1432 for**: Theoretical motivation — financial-sector stress should propagate to broader market via balance-sheet / liquidity channels. Justifies including cross-sectional bank vol / dispersion as systemic stress proxies.
- **Connection to K757b/bv2**: K757bv2 estimated CoVaR on TW banks → TSMC and found significant contemporaneous spillover. K1432 tests whether the leading component of this CoVaR-style relation gives OOS forecasting power for TSMC volatility (it does not).
- **Why the NULL is informative**: Adrian-Brunnermeier CoVaR is a contemporaneous tail-dependence measure; its predictive (rather than concurrent) value at the daily horizon is genuinely an open question. K1432 contributes a NULL for the TW daily case.
