# K1428 References

1. Leushuis, V., & Petkov, N. (2025). *Advances in forecasting realized volatility: a review of methodologies*. Financial Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40854-025-00809-5
   - 2025 review centered on realized-volatility forecasting model classes.
   - Key use in K1428: model taxonomy, scope of reviewed literature, and explicit future-research gaps.

2. Gunnarsson, E. S., Isern, H. R., Kaloudis, A., Risstad, M., Vigdel, B., & Westgaard, S. (2024). *Prediction of realized volatility and implied volatility indices using AI and machine learning: A review*. International Review of Financial Analysis. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.irfa.2024.103221
   - AI/ML-focused review.
   - Key use in K1428: no blanket evidence that AI universally dominates; XAI usage remains limited.

3. Bucci, A. (2018). *Forecasting realized volatility: A review*. Journal of the Korean Statistical Society, 47(4), 395-404. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jkss.2018.08.002
   - Older canonical review covering HAR, MIDAS, realized GARCH, HEAVY, jumps, breaks, and evaluation methods.
   - Key use in K1428: baseline map of what was already known before the 2025 wave.

4. Branco, R. R., Rubesam, A., & Zevallos, M. (2024). *Forecasting realized volatility: Does anything beat linear models?* Journal of Empirical Finance. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jempfin.2024.101524
   - Ten global stock indices, 2000-2021.
   - Key use in K1428: additional predictors help daily/weekly forecasts, but nonlinear ML does not generally achieve statistical dominance over linear benchmarks.

5. Skintzi, V., & Fameliti, S. P. (2025). *Combining realized volatility estimators based on economic performance*. Journal of Asset Management. https://doi.org/10.1057/s41260-025-00415-1
   - Forecast-combination paper evaluated through economic criteria.
   - Key use in K1428: economic-value-aware combinations can outperform purely statistical combinations, especially in high-volatility periods.

6. Akgun, O. B., & Gulay, E. (2025). *Dynamics in Realized Volatility Forecasting: Evaluating GARCH Models and Deep Learning Algorithms Across Parameter Variations*. Computational Economics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10614-024-10694-2
   - Key use in K1428: realized-measure choice interacts with model class; 5-minute RV and MedRV need to be treated as serious design choices rather than preprocessing footnotes.
