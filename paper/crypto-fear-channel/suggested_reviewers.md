## Suggested Reviewers (5 candidates)

### 1. Francis X. Diebold — University of Pennsylvania

- **Email**: fdiebold@sas.upenn.edu
- **Expertise**: Volatility spillover index, vector autoregression connectedness, forecast evaluation
- **Why suitable**: Diebold and Yilmaz (2009, 2012, 2014) provide the spillover-index methodology that constitutes one of the paper's four core building blocks (\S4.4). The §5.3 reframing of Bitcoin from fear "originator" to "amplifier" rests directly on the net-receiver decomposition Diebold's framework supplies. As the canonical author of the methodology, Diebold is the most authoritative single referee for assessing whether the directional spillover analysis is fair to the original framework's intent and for evaluating the §6.1 rolling-window stability robustness.
- **Conflict check**: No coauthorship with Yi-Hao Lai. Different country / institution.

### 2. Larisa Yarovaya — University of Southampton

- **Email**: l.yarovaya@soton.ac.uk
- **Expertise**: Cryptocurrency contagion, COVID-19 financial transmission, equity-crypto cross-asset spillover
- **Why suitable**: Yarovaya, Brzeszczyński, Goodell, Lucey, and Lau (2022) is a key reference for our §1 motivation, as it documents the intensification of information transmission between cryptocurrency and traditional asset markets during the pandemic — directly relevant to our §5.3 finding that COVID-2020 is a structural watershed. Yarovaya is one of the most prolific researchers in cryptocurrency-equity contagion and has written extensively on the asymmetric and regime-dependent transmission channels we document.
- **Conflict check**: No coauthorship with Yi-Hao Lai. Different country / institution.

### 3. Tony Klein — Queen's University Belfast (formerly Open University)

- **Email**: tony.klein@qub.ac.uk
- **Expertise**: Cryptocurrency volatility, Bitcoin-gold safe-haven comparisons, portfolio risk
- **Why suitable**: Klein, Pham Thu, and Walther (2018) "Bitcoin is not the New Gold" is a key reference in our §2.1 safe-haven debate framing. Klein has subsequently published on cryptocurrency volatility dynamics and the comparative risk profile of crypto vs.\ traditional safe-haven assets. The paper's finding that BTC is a *net receiver* of fear (rather than a hedge) directly engages the safe-haven literature Klein has helped define, making him well-positioned to assess whether our reframing is fair to the broader debate.
- **Conflict check**: No coauthorship with Yi-Hao Lai. Different country / institution.

### 4. Brian Lucey — Trinity College Dublin

- **Email**: blucey@tcd.ie
- **Expertise**: Commodity markets, cryptocurrency-commodity spillover, behavioral finance
- **Why suitable**: Lucey is co-author on multiple key references in the paper's literature review (Bouri et al.\ 2020; Corbet et al.\ 2018; Akyildirim et al.\ 2020; Yarovaya et al.\ 2022; Shahzad et al.\ 2019). His breadth across the cryptocurrency-equity spillover literature — particularly the safe-haven and crisis-period transmission threads — makes him the most informed referee on whether our three stylized facts genuinely add to the existing literature or recapitulate established findings. **Possible mild conflict via co-authorship cluster**: see "Opposed reviewers" notes.
- **Conflict check**: No direct coauthorship with Yi-Hao Lai. Different country / institution.

### 5. Sébastien Laurent — Aix-Marseille School of Economics

- **Email**: sebastien.laurent@univ-amu.fr
- **Expertise**: Volatility econometrics, multivariate GARCH, forecast comparison, asymmetric tests
- **Why suitable**: Laurent has published extensively on volatility econometrics (high-frequency RV, GARCH, copula-based extreme dependence), with particular methodological depth on the asymmetric and regime-conditional tests we deploy. The combination of methodological rigor + cross-asset scope makes him a strong fit for assessing the paper's methodology section (§4) and the OOS forecasting evaluation (§7). He is also less likely to be in the cryptocurrency-Lucey co-authorship cluster, providing referee diversity.
- **Conflict check**: No coauthorship with Yi-Hao Lai. Different country / institution.
