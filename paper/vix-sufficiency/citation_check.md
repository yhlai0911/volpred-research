### 19. Harvey, Liu, and Zhu (2016) -- `harvey2016`
- **Cited as**: Harvey, C. R., Liu, Y., & Zhu, H. (2016). ...and the cross-section of expected returns. *Review of Financial Studies*, 29(1), 5--68.
- **Actual**: Correct. RFS Vol. 29, No. 1, pp. 5-68.
- **Usage in text**: Cited extensively for the $|t|>3.0$ threshold.
- **Issue**: **HIGH -- Misrepresentation of Harvey et al. recommendation.** The paper states Harvey et al. "recommend $|t|>3.0$ as a minimum threshold for asset pricing tests" (Section 5.2). However, Harvey et al. (2016) actually present a more nuanced argument:
  - Their recommended threshold depends on the number of factors tested and the assumed prior probability
  - The $t>3.0$ number is from their Table 1 but corresponds to specific assumptions about the number of tested strategies (~300 in their context)
  - For 11 tests (as in this paper), a simple Bonferroni gives $t>3.23$ which is close but not identical
  - Harvey et al. advocate a Bayesian framework with explicit priors, not just raising the t-threshold
  - The paper should note that $|t|>3.0$ is an approximate conservative threshold inspired by Harvey et al., not their literal recommendation for all contexts
- **Status**: **FAIL (HIGH)** -- Oversimplified attribution of the $|t|>3.0$ rule
