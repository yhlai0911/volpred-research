\section{H1: Non-Comparable Evaluation Periods}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\textbf{Problem.} Table~3 (v1) compared strategies across different time periods (BH 2010--2026, GJR VT 2020--2026, 8.63/VIX 2016--2026), making Sharpe ratio rankings unreliable.

\textbf{Changes:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \ADD{New Table 4 (``Common-Period Strategy Comparison, 2020--2026'')} evaluates \textit{all five} strategies over the identical 2020--2026 window. Over this common period:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item GJR VT: 1.108 (unchanged, this was already its native period)
  \item GARCH VT: 0.994
  \item EWMA VT: 0.927 (up from 0.796 over 2010--2026)
  \item 8.63/VIX: 0.856 (up from 0.690 over 2016--2026)
  \item Buy \& Hold: 0.824 (up from 0.729 over 2010--2026)
  \end{itemize}
\item \MOD{Table 3 notes} updated with cross-reference to Table 4 and stronger caveat language.
\item \ADD{Explanatory paragraph} between Tables 3 and 4 documents that the Sharpe gap between GJR VT and 8.63/VIX narrows from 0.418 to 0.252 over the common period, confirming that period effects explain a substantial portion of the apparent superiority.
\item \MOD{Abstract, Introduction, Conclusion} updated to reference common-period results.
\end{itemize}

\section{H3: Diversification Amplification ($N = 10 \to N = 9$)}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\textbf{Problem.} The 4.6$\times$ amplification ratio was based on only 10 securities, one of which (0056.TW) is an ETF misclassified as an ``individual stock.''

\textbf{Changes:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \MOD{0056.TW reclassified.} Data section now reads ``Nine individual Taiwan stocks and one ETF.'' 0056.TW labeled as ETF throughout.
\item \MOD{Tables 1 and 2} updated:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item 0056.TW separated into its own row under ``ETF (excluded from individual-stock average).''
  \item New row: ``9-stock average (excl.\ 0056)'' with $\bar{\gamma} = 0.054$.
  \item Existing row relabeled: ``10-security average (incl.\ 0056 ETF)'' with $\bar{\gamma} = 0.060$.
  \item Table notes explain why 0056 is excluded from the primary average.
  \end{itemize}
\item \MOD{Amplification ratio recalculated:}
  \begin{itemize}
  \item Primary: $0.272 / 0.054 = 5.0\times$ (9 stocks, excluding 0056 ETF)
  \item Secondary: $0.272 / 0.060 = 4.5\times$ (10 securities, including 0056 ETF)
  \item 0050.TW-based: $0.087 / 0.054 = 1.6\times$ (was $1.45\times$ with 0056)
  \end{itemize}
\item \ADD{Bootstrap confidence interval.} New paragraph reports 90\% CI from block bootstrap (10,000 reps, block = 252 days): $[2.8, \, 8.1]$ with median 4.9. The CI excludes 1.0 but includes the U.S.\ benchmark at its lower bound.
\item \ADD{``Sensitivity to 0056.TW inclusion'' paragraph.} Documents that including 0056 biases the ratio downward (conservative direction) and is factually incorrect.
\item \MOD{Language downgraded:} ``4.6$\times$'' $\to$ ``approximately 5.0$\times$ (suggestive evidence)'' throughout abstract, introduction, discussion, and conclusion.
\item \MOD{Abstract} now includes CI and $N$ in parenthetical.
\item \MOD{Conclusion} explicitly acknowledges wide confidence interval as a limitation.
\end{itemize}
