\item \LOW\ \textbf{L2: $N(0, \sigma_f)$ should be $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_f^2)$.}

\textbf{Round~3 status}: \OPEN

Eq.~(1) (line~85) still uses $N(0, \sigma_f)$ and line~93 uses $N(0, 0.3)$. Both should use $\mathcal{N}$ (calligraphic) to distinguish from agent count~$N$. Also, $N(0, \sigma_f)$ is ambiguous---it could mean variance $\sigma_f$ or standard deviation $\sigma_f$. Standard notation is $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_f^2)$ for a distribution with standard deviation $\sigma_f$, or $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma)$ if $\sigma$ is explicitly defined as the standard deviation. Since $\sigma_f = 0.16/\sqrt{252}$ is already a standard deviation, the correct notation is $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_f^2)$.

\section*{Overall Assessment}

\textbf{Verdict: Minor Revision}

The v1.5 manuscript has resolved all HIGH-severity issues identified across Rounds~1--2. The two most critical improvements are: (1)~the explicit definition of $\Delta\sigma^{\text{real}}_t = \max(0,\, \hat{\sigma}^{20d}_t - 0.16)$ with its economic rationale, which brings the paper's key equation into alignment with the simulation code; and (2)~the consistent OAT wording across abstract, introduction, results, and conclusion. Five MED issues remain, all addressable in a single editing pass without re-running any simulations. The most impactful is expanding the reference list to $\geq$~18 entries---this is straightforward and would eliminate a likely first-round reviewer complaint. The other four (bias direction, convergence note, adoption sourcing, equation footnote) are each one-sentence fixes.

The paper's core contribution---quantifying the VT crowding tipping point via ABM with a fixed-liquidity design validation---remains sound, well-documented, and timely. With the five MED fixes listed above, the paper would be ready for submission to \emph{Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control} or \emph{Journal of Financial Stability}.
