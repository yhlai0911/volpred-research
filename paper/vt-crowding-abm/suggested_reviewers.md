Most FRL submission portals allow 3–5 suggested reviewers and 0–2 opposed (do-not-consider) reviewers. The list below is sized to fit either norm.

## Suggested Reviewers (5 candidates)

### 1. Rama Cont — Imperial College London (also Oxford-Man Institute)

- **Email**: r.cont@imperial.ac.uk
- **Expertise**: Agent-based finance, herd behavior, systemic risk, fire sales, market microstructure
- **Why suitable**: Cont and Bouchaud (2000) is one of the paper's core references — the herd-behavior aggregate-fluctuations ABM framework that P5 extends to a multi-strategy threshold setting. Cont has subsequently published on fire-sale dynamics (Cont & Schaanning 2017), liquidity feedback (Cont & Wagalath 2013), and stress-testing under correlated trading — all directly germane to P5's positive-feedback crowding mechanism.
- **Conflict check**: No coauthorship with Yi-Hao Lai. Different country / institution.

### 2. Blake LeBaron — Brandeis University

- **Email**: blebaron@brandeis.edu
- **Expertise**: Agent-based computational economics, financial market modeling, heterogeneous-agent models
- **Why suitable**: LeBaron (2006) Handbook of Computational Economics chapter is cited as one of P5's foundational ABM references. LeBaron is among the canonical authors in agent-based finance and has decades of publication on calibration, validation, and cross-strategy interaction in heterogeneous-agent markets — exactly the methodological space P5 occupies.
- **Conflict check**: No coauthorship with Yi-Hao Lai. Different country / institution.

### 3. Fabrizio Lillo — University of Pisa

- **Email**: fabrizio.lillo@unipi.it
- **Expertise**: Market microstructure, agent-based models, financial fragility, cross-impact
- **Why suitable**: Lillo has published extensively on ABM-based market-impact models, cross-asset trading-flow propagation, and stress-test designs. The paper's Phase 2b microstructure-OAT design (Kyle-λ ±50% sensitivity) is the kind of cross-impact / liquidity-stress robustness analysis Lillo regularly evaluates as referee.
- **Conflict check**: No coauthorship with Yi-Hao Lai. Different country / institution.

### 4. J. Doyne Farmer — University of Oxford (INET) / Santa Fe Institute

- **Email**: doyne.farmer@oxford.edu (or doyne.farmer@inet.ox.ac.uk)
- **Expertise**: Agent-based finance, complexity economics, systemic risk, financial network modeling
- **Why suitable**: Farmer co-led the Oxford INET ABM group on systemic risk during the 2010s and has co-published with Bookstaber on financial vulnerability modeling — closely overlapping the systemic-risk framing of P5. Farmer's cross-disciplinary expertise (physics-rooted ABM + finance) maps onto P5's Kyle-microstructure + multi-strategy-class design.
- **Conflict check**: No coauthorship with Yi-Hao Lai. Different country / institution.

### 5. Andrei A. Kirilenko — University of Cambridge (Cambridge Centre for Finance)

- **Email**: a.kirilenko@jbs.cam.ac.uk
- **Expertise**: High-frequency trading, market microstructure, flash crashes, regulatory perspectives on ABM
- **Why suitable**: Kirilenko (formerly CFTC chief economist, post-2010-Flash-Crash) is one of the few senior referees who combines academic ABM expertise with regulatory / systemic-risk policy framing — the natural audience for P5's §5.1 policy implications and its argument for monitoring combined positive-feedback adoption.
- **Conflict check**: No coauthorship with Yi-Hao Lai. Different country / institution.

## Why these five (rationale for editor)

The five suggested referees cover the paper's three methodological pillars:

| Pillar | Primary referee | Backup |
|---|---|---|
| Agent-based finance methodology | LeBaron | Cont, Farmer |
| Systemic risk / financial fragility | Cont, Farmer | Kirilenko |
| Market microstructure (Kyle / λ-γ OAT) | Lillo | Cont |
| Crowding / multi-strategy interactions | Cont, Lillo | LeBaron |
| Policy / regulatory framing (§5.1) | Kirilenko | Farmer |

No two referees from the same institution or country. All five are tenured / senior researchers with substantive methodological-letter publications, appropriate for FRL editorial assignment.

The reviewer pool intentionally **skews toward European institutions** because the U.S. ABM-finance senior pool is thin (Bookstaber retired from OFR; Tivnan now industry; Paddrik regulatory) and would otherwise overlap with the GARCH-econometrics pool used for P6 PRG. Drawing reviewers from a distinct subspecialty community avoids portfolio-wide reviewer overlap as the 9-paper portfolio rolls out toward submission.
