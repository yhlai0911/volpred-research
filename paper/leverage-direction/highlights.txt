Highlights

- Gold's inverted leverage is regime-dependent: unconditional rolling mean is indistinguishable from zero (gamma = +0.002), but fear-driven rallies vs liquidation declines differ at t = -3.79 (p < 0.001)
- GJR-GARCH beats symmetric GARCH only when gamma is statistically significant (t > 1.65); 11/11 in-sample and 6/6 out-of-sample model comparisons correctly classified
- Gamma direction predicts the channel of volatility-targeting alpha for equity-type assets (Spearman rho = 0.886, p = 0.019)
- Volatility-targeting drawdown reduction is universal across asset classes and driven by base volatility level (rho = 0.944), independent of gamma sign
- US-to-Asia time-zone momentum yields t-statistics exceeding the Harvey et al. (2016) multiple-testing threshold in six Asia-Pacific markets, net of transaction costs
