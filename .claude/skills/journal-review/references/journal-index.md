## Quick routing heuristics
- Core = forecasting + OOS evaluation → **IJF** (premier) or **JoF** (fast, free-format).
- Core = new estimator/test/inference theory → **JoE**.
- General empirical finance with policy/institutional angle → **JBF**.
- Top-3 ambition, strong economic mechanism + identification → **JFE** / **RFS**.
- One sharp novel result, short → **FRL** (not a single-country replication).
- Asia-Pacific data (TAIFEX/Nikkei/HSI/KOSPI) is the setting → **PBFJ**.
- Implementable investment-decision payoff, practitioner audience → **JPM** (allocation focus) / **FAJ** (broader, flagship).
- **Applied econometrics with substantive economic case + willing to satisfy data archive** → **JAE**.
- **New estimator/test with simulation + business/econ application** → **JBES**.
- **Rigorous empirical finance, standard methods, no policy angle needed** → **JEmpFin** (higher bar than JBF on empirical rigor; lower on policy framing).
- **Financial econometrics — methodology + finance application paired** → **JFinEc** (dedicated venue; replicability review on acceptance).
- **Quantitative-methods lens (derivatives, ML, portfolio, algorithms)** → **QF** (single-blind; math-heavy).
- **Applied empirical result, cross-country/panel, no methodology twist** → **EmpEcon** (receptive second-tier fallback).
- **Empirical finance with European or international angle + willing to pay $600 for 4-wk decision** → **EFM**.
- **Nonlinear/regime-switching/chaos methods** central to the contribution → **SNDE** (free OA; niche audience).
