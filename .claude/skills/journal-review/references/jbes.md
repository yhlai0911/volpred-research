# Journal of Business & Economic Statistics (JBES)

- **Abbrev / ISSN**: JBES (J. Bus. Econ. Stat.); ISSN 0735-0015 (print), 1537-2707 (online)
- **Publisher**: Taylor & Francis, on behalf of American Statistical Association (ASA). Submit via ScholarOne Manuscripts.
- **Tier**: Top-field applied econometrics/statistics (ABS/AJG 4, ABDC A*, SSCI Q1). Considered the leading North-American venue for methodological econometrics with substantive business/economics application; sister journal to JAE and JoE.

## Scope
NEW methodological research in statistics and econometrics with substantive applications in economics, business, and finance. Emphasizes methods with impact on applied practice (as opposed to purely theoretical work in JoE).
- **Fits**: new estimators/tests for time-series (volatility, cointegration, structural breaks), panel dynamics, forecast evaluation, quantile methods, machine-learning-in-econometrics, high-dimensional finance, causal inference in finance.
- **Weak fit**: papers whose contribution is purely applied with no methodology innovation (→ JAE/JEmpFin), theoretical statistics with no economic/business motivation, empirical corporate finance without econometric novelty.

## Format
- **Length**: no hard page limit; typical published papers 25-40 pages double-spaced. Long online appendix strongly encouraged for supplementary results/proofs.
- **Abstract**: unstructured, ≤150 words typical.
- **Keywords/JEL**: 3-6 keywords + JEL codes required.
- **Structure**: IMRaD; anonymized main file (double-blind); separate title page.
- **References**: author-date; JASA style or similar at acceptance; DOIs where available.
- **Figures/tables**: editable; equations numbered; supplementary online appendix for derivations, extra tables, replication notes.
- **LaTeX / Word**: both accepted; Taylor & Francis LaTeX template at revision.

## Submission process
ScholarOne Manuscripts (via Taylor & Francis portal). **No submission fee**. Required: anonymized manuscript, separate title page, abstract ≤150 w, keywords, JEL codes, cover letter, code/data statement. ASA member discounts on APC (if gold OA chosen).

## Review model
Double-anonymized. Editor + associate editor screening → 1-3 referees → decision. Median time to first decision ~4-6 months (estimate based on published editorial reports; unverified for 2025). Desk-reject common for methodology-thin submissions.

## Fees & OA
No submission fee. Hybrid: subscription route free; gold OA APC USD 3,175 (Taylor & Francis 2025 rate, verify live). ASA members and institutions with Read & Publish agreements may qualify for waiver.

## Distinctive requirements
1. **Methodology contribution is non-negotiable**: reviewers explicitly weight "novelty of statistical/econometric method" as primary criterion. Application-first papers get desk-rejected to JAE/JEmpFin.
2. **Reproducibility policy** (ASA/T&F): code + data required on acceptance for publication; hosted in T&F's supplementary materials repository or public archive (GitHub/Zenodo).
3. Long online supplementary appendix ENCOURAGED (proofs, simulations, extra tables) — keeps main text focused.
4. Simulation study expected for any new estimator/test (size/power/coverage under multiple DGPs).
5. **ORCID required** at submission (ASA policy).
6. LaTeX preferred for math-heavy submissions.

## Pre-submission checklist (extends generic)
- Abstract ≤150 words, unstructured, no refs.
- 3-6 keywords + JEL codes on title page.
- Main file anonymized (names, affiliations, self-cites in 3rd person, acknowledgements stripped); title page SEPARATE.
- **Methodology contribution stated in 1st paragraph of intro** — reviewer will look for this before reading further.
- **Simulation study section** (if proposing new estimator/test): finite-sample size, power under alternatives, comparison to closest competitor, at least 2-3 DGPs.
- Empirical application illustrates the METHOD's payoff, not the other way around.
- Code + data package ready (README, fixed seeds, pinned environment); can be private link at submission, public on acceptance.
- Econometric rigor: correct OOS design, lagged signals, DM/HLN/SPA + HAC, QLIKE = actual/predicted, cluster-robust inference, no asset-day iid pooling.
- Multiple-testing control (Reality Check/SPA/Romano-Wolf/MCS) for any "significant" claim.
- Robustness: multiple periods, alt specs, sensitivity to tuning parameters.
- Position vs recent JBES + JoE + JAE; 2-3 concrete contributions; claim strength matched to evidence.
- Author-date refs + DOIs; supplementary appendix for long derivations.
