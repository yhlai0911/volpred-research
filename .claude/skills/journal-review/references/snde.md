# Studies in Nonlinear Dynamics & Econometrics (SNDE)

- **Abbrev / ISSN**: SNDE (Stud. Nonlinear Dyn. Econom.); ISSN 1081-1826 (print), 1558-3708 (online)
- **Publisher**: De Gruyter (Brill | De Gruyter; open-access via Subscribe to Open, S2O). Sponsored by The Society for Nonlinear Dynamics and Econometrics. Submit via Manuscript Central (ScholarOne): http://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/dgsnde.
- **Tier**: Second-tier specialized (ABS/AJG 2, ABDC B, SSCI Q2). Niche but respected: THE dedicated venue for nonlinear econometric methods applied to economics and finance. Low competition in regime-switching/structural-break/chaos/threshold econometrics.
- **IF 2024**: 0.9 (CiteScore 2024: 1.4) | **Quartile**: SSCI Q2 (Economics), SJR Q3 (Economics, Econometrics and Finance)
- **URL**: https://www.degruyterbrill.com/journal/key/snde/html

## Scope
Econometrics and dynamical systems theory applied to economic and financial markets. Publishes both theoretical (method/estimator) and empirical (application) papers. Explicit emphasis on reproducibility: data sets, algorithms, and programs are disseminated so other scholars can replicate results.
- **Fits**: threshold/regime-switching GARCH and VAR models, nonlinear time series (TAR, STAR, MS-GARCH), structural break detection in volatility, chaos and complexity in financial markets, agent-based models, nonlinear cointegration, regime-conditional volatility forecasting, bootstrap inference in nonlinear settings.
- **Weak fit**: linear econometrics with standard methods (→ JAE/EmpEcon), general empirical finance without nonlinear/regime dimension (→ JBF/JEmpFin), pure financial theory (→ JFE/RFS), mathematical option pricing (→ QF).

## Format
- **Length**: no explicit hard cap; typical papers 25-40 pages. Conciseness valued.
- **Abstract**: unstructured; typically ≤200 words (no strict count stated; keep ≤150 to be safe).
- **Keywords/JEL**: 4-6 keywords; JEL codes recommended.
- **Structure**: IMRaD; double-anonymized submission required (see review model).
- **References**: De Gruyter standard; author-date format; DOIs required.
- **Figures/tables**: editable; color figures accepted; equations numbered.
- **LaTeX / Word**: both accepted; De Gruyter provides LaTeX template.
- **Ethical/Legal Declarations**: separate Word file upload required at submission (De Gruyter policy).

## Submission process
Manuscript Central (http://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/dgsnde). **No submission fee** (Subscribe to Open model — APC covered by S2O consortia). Required: anonymized manuscript, abstract, keywords, ethical/legal declarations file, data-availability statement. Best Paper Award: USD 500 annually since 2015 (eligibility automatic).

## Review model
Double-anonymized. Editor screens → typically 2 referees → decision. First-decision time not officially published; community experience ~3-5 months (unverified). Acceptance rate not publicly disclosed; estimated ~20-25% based on journal volume and submission rate (lower-tier niche journals typically higher acceptance than Q1 journals).

## Fees & OA
No submission fee. Open access via **Subscribe to Open (S2O)** — all accepted articles published under Creative Commons license at no cost to authors when S2O consortium reaches funding target; no APC for authors (as of 2024 confirmed). If S2O target not met in a given year, articles fall back to subscription. Verify current S2O status before submission.

## Distinctive requirements
1. **Nonlinear / regime / dynamical systems component is MANDATORY**: submissions must involve nonlinear structure (threshold, regime-switching, structural break, chaos, agent-based). Linear GARCH with standard evaluation → JEmpFin/JBF.
2. **Free open access for authors** via S2O model — no APC. Strong incentive for authors from institutions without OA agreements.
3. **Replication-first culture**: journal explicitly disseminates data + algorithms. Authors expected to supply replication materials.
4. **Ethical/Legal Declarations template** must be uploaded as separate Word file at submission (De Gruyter administrative requirement).
5. **Best Paper Award USD 500** — low-cost upside vs higher-IF journals.
6. Quarterly journal with modest volume — each accepted paper receives proportionally more attention in the community.

## Pre-submission checklist (extends generic)
- Abstract ≤150 words (conservative target), unstructured, no refs; 4-6 keywords + JEL codes.
- Manuscript fully anonymized (double-blind); separate title page.
- **Nonlinear/regime component clearly stated in intro and abstract** — reviewers are specialists in nonlinear dynamics; they will check immediately.
- Complete Ethical/Legal Declarations Word file (required by De Gruyter).
- Data-availability statement + replication package (data + code + README; fixed seeds; reproducible from clean environment).
- Confirm S2O open-access status for current year (check De Gruyter journal page).
- Econometric rigor: correct OOS design, lagged signals (`signal.shift(1)`), DM/HLN/SPA + HAC, QLIKE = actual/predicted; horizon-matched inference.
- For regime-based claims: regime identification must be robust (LR test, bootstrap, out-of-sample validation across multiple regimes); no "pre-specifying" regime boundaries that benefit one model.
- Multiple-testing control (Reality Check/SPA/Romano-Wolf/MCS); no "only" or "unique" claim without re-verifying.
- Robustness: multiple periods incl. crisis/bear, alt regime specs, sensitivity to threshold/bandwidth choices.
- Position vs recent SNDE + adjacent (Journal of Time Series Analysis, JBES, JFinEc) — cite SNDE papers.
- Author-date refs + DOIs; De Gruyter house style at revision.
