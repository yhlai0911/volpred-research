# Journal of Econometrics (JoE)

- **Abbrev**: JoE (J. Econom.); ISSN 0304-4076
- **Publisher**: Elsevier (North-Holland); editorial ops via co-editors' site/portal.
- **Tier**: Top field journal in econometrics (#1-2 dedicated econometrics outlet; peer of Econometrica on methods). For a vol-forecasting group it is a **methods-first** target, not a general finance journal.

## Scope
Important, high-quality NEW research in theoretical AND applied econometrics: identification, estimation, testing, decision, prediction, inference. Classical + Bayesian + experimental design + ML methods in scope.
- **Defining filter**: a genuine ECONOMETRIC-METHODOLOGY contribution (handling editor screens for a real advance). Applied-only papers (no methodological novelty) are typically desk-rejected even if well executed; applied illustration must demonstrate/motivate the method, not stand alone.
- **Fits (vol group)**: new forecasting/loss-evaluation/comparative-predictive-ability theory w/ rigorous asymptotics — NOT "we applied GARCH to asset X".

## Format
- **Length**: no hard cap; checklist asks main text "~40 pages in ≥1.5 spacing and 11pt". Overlong discouraged; proofs/extra results in appendix/supplement. Accepted ~8k-12k words (indicative).
- **Abstract**: unstructured, max ~250 words; stands alone; avoid citations.
- **Keywords/JEL**: 1-7 keywords; include JEL on title page (conventional for econ journal; confirm live).
- **Structure**: unstructured — Intro, model/assumptions, theory (identification/estimation/asymptotics), Monte Carlo, empirical illustration, conclusion, appendices/proofs. State assumptions formally + provide proofs.
- **References**: **author-date (Harvard)** — NOT numbered Vancouver (some 3rd-party templates wrongly show numbered).
- **Figures/tables**: editable source for whole submission incl. figures/tables; tables editable text; production-quality figures numbered+captioned. PDF only for the initial PDF-only file; editable source on acceptance.
- **LaTeX**: elsarticle.cls + BibTeX; submit .tex (PDF generated for review); PDF not acceptable as final source; LaTeX may be double-column, Word single-column.

## Submission process
Submit online + **pay $75 nonrefundable fee + upload proof of payment**. **PORTAL IN TRANSITION**: historic Editorial Express now states "resubmissions only"; new submissions appear to route to Elsevier Editorial Manager — CONFIRM the live new-submission portal on the current Elsevier JoE page before submitting. Initial submission = single PDF (manuscript + cover letter + supporting); editable source later. Cover letter states the methodology contribution + chosen track (Regular / Annals / Themed). Full author list w/ standard institution names (research-integrity). Suggested/opposed reviewers may be offered. Declarations: COI, funding (name grants), CRediT, generative-AI use.

## Review model
Handling/co-editor methodology screen FIRST (most early rejections here), then ≥2 referees if passed. Blinding UNCERTAIN (historic Elsevier/econ norm single-blind; one source claims double-blind) — prepare a manuscript not dependent on anonymity. First decision median ~87 d / mean ~95 d (ASSA); full cycles run many months to 1-2 yr. Acceptance ~10% or lower (approximate).

## Fees & OA
$75 submission fee (nonrefundable; charged on new submissions + resubmissions > 1 yr after original decision; no student discount; EU VAT; proof uploaded). Hybrid: subscription free; optional OA APC ~US$5,030 excl. tax (verify live). 

## Distinctive requirements
1. $75 fee + proof of payment is a hard gate. 2. Initial = SINGLE PDF; editable .tex/.docx mandatory on acceptance. 3. ~40-pp / ≥1.5-spacing / 11pt expectation. 4. Decisive gate = genuine METHODOLOGY contribution (applied-only screened out pre-referee). 5. Author-date refs, NOT numbered. 6. Data-availability statement + replication/code package for any empirical/computational illustration. 7. CRediT + COI + funding + AI declarations. 8. Select correct track (Regular/Annals/Themed). Highlights + graphical abstract OPTIONAL.

## Pre-submission checklist (extends generic)
- Lead with a clear, novel ECONOMETRIC-METHODOLOGY contribution; if "apply existing method to data" → reframe around the method or send elsewhere (JAE/IJF/JFE).
- State assumptions formally + full asymptotic theory w/ proofs (consistency, limiting distribution, inference validity); regularity conditions stated; long proofs in appendix/supplement.
- Monte Carlo on finite-sample size/power (or forecast accuracy), seeded, DGPs + sample sizes described.
- Forecast-eval care (vol): DM / Giacomini-White / Hansen MCS / proper QLIKE (actual/predicted), HAC/HLN corrections, target-aligned forecasts (no look-ahead, no same-index origin/target mismatch).
- Inference honesty: no asset-day iid pooling — cluster-robust / panel-HAC / date-aggregated loss; no overstated uniqueness after HAC retrofit.
- Format: main ~40 pp, ≥1.5 spacing, 11pt; single PDF; editable .tex (elsarticle) ready for acceptance.
- References author-date (Harvard), alphabetical — NOT numbered.
- Abstract ≤250 w stands alone (no citations); 1-7 keywords; JEL on title page.
- Replication: DAS + clean runnable package (seeds fixed, README, sources/periods/sample sizes documented).
- Declarations: cover letter (methodology contribution + track), CRediT, COI, funding, AI-use.
- Confirm CURRENT new-submission portal (Editorial Express = resubmissions-only); pay $75 + upload proof.
- Benchmark vs the econometrics literature (not just finance); make the comparative advantage over existing estimators/tests explicit + theory-backed.
