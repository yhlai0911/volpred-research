# Journal of Empirical Finance (JEmpFin)

- **Abbrev / ISSN**: JEmpFin (J. Empir. Finance); ISSN 0927-5398
- **Publisher**: Elsevier (ScienceDirect; hybrid, supports OA). Submit via Editorial Manager.
- **Tier**: Top-field empirical finance (ABS/AJG 3, ABDC A, SSCI Q1). Focused on rigorous empirical work in finance — a natural fit between JBF (broader institutional/policy) and JoE (pure econometric methodology).

## Scope
Empirical research in finance: asset pricing, market microstructure, corporate finance with strong empirical design, risk management, volatility, derivatives, international finance. Emphasis on rigorous empirical methodology applied to financial questions.
- **Fits**: OOS forecasting of volatility/returns, VaR/ES backtesting, HAR/GARCH-family evaluation, high-frequency data, factor models, event studies, empirical asset pricing anomalies, portfolio strategy backtests with proper controls.
- **Weak fit**: pure theory (→ JFinEc if quant-methods, → JF/JFE if economic theory), pure methodology w/o finance application (→ JoE/JBES), single-country descriptive papers, pure computational/mathematical finance (→ QF).

## Format
- **Length**: no hard cap; field norm ~30-45 pages double-spaced. Reviewer bar is low tolerance for filler.
- **Abstract**: unstructured, ≤150 words hard cap (Elsevier default).
- **Keywords/JEL**: 3-6 keywords + JEL codes required.
- **Structure**: IMRaD; anonymized main file (double-blind); separate title page.
- **References**: author-date Harvard (Elsevier style); DOIs where available.
- **Figures/tables**: editable; equations numbered; appendices A/B; graphical abstract optional.
- **LaTeX**: elsarticle encouraged; editable source required (.tex or single-column .docx); PDF not acceptable as source.

## Submission process
Editorial Manager. **No submission fee** (confirmed as of 2025; verify live before submission — Elsevier finance titles have periodically introduced fees). Required: anonymized manuscript, separate title page, abstract ≤150 w, 3-6 keywords + JEL codes, data-availability statement, declarations (COI/funding/AI/CRediT).

## Review model
Double-anonymized. Editor screens (~40% desk-reject with no reviewer feedback) → 1-2 reviewers → decision. Reported acceptance rate ~15% (85% overall rejection per journal's editor communications). Typical first decision 2-4 months.

## Fees & OA
No submission fee (verify live). Hybrid: subscription route free; gold OA APC USD 3,200 (Elsevier 2025 rate, excl. tax; verify live). Elsevier waivers via Research4Life / institutional agreements.

## Distinctive requirements
1. **Empirical rigor is the gate**: OOS design, robust standard errors, multiple-testing awareness, robustness to reasonable perturbations. Reviewers explicitly asked to check for data snooping.
2. **High desk-reject rate for scope misfit**: papers better suited to Accounting, Computational/Mathematical Finance, Operations Research, Statistics, or Econometrics journals are desk-rejected without review.
3. **Data-availability statement** required (Elsevier data policy); code/data deposit encouraged (not mandatory unless method-focused).
4. Fully anonymized submission (Elsevier double-blind).
5. Generative-AI declaration section required if AI was used in preparation (Elsevier 2024 policy).
6. CRediT authorship statement.
7. Editable source required (no PDF as manuscript).

## Pre-submission checklist (extends generic)
- Abstract ≤150 words, unstructured, no refs (count it).
- 3-6 keywords + JEL codes on title page.
- Manuscript fully anonymized (names, affiliations, self-cites 3rd person, acknowledgements stripped); title page SEPARATE.
- Data-availability statement included; code/data package prepared even if not required (reviewers will ask).
- Editable source (.tex elsarticle or single-column .docx) — never PDF.
- Complete declarations (COI/funding/AI/CRediT).
- **Fit check FIRST** — is this really empirical finance, or does it belong to a methodology/accounting/computational journal? Scope misfit = desk-reject.
- Econometric rigor: correct OOS design, lagged signals (`signal.shift(1)`), DM/HLN/SPA + HAC, QLIKE = actual/predicted, cluster-robust or date-aggregated inference (no asset-day iid pooling).
- Multiple-testing/data-snooping control (Reality Check/SPA/Romano-Wolf/MCS); no "unique" or "only" claim without re-verifying current results table.
- Robustness: multiple periods incl. crisis/bear subsample, alt specs, subsample stability, hyperparameter sensitivity (dedicated section).
- Position vs recent JEmpFin + top-finance; 2-3 concrete contributions; claim strength matches evidence.
- Author-date Harvard refs + DOIs; verify all stats reproducible from code.
