# European Financial Management (EFM)

- **Abbrev / ISSN**: EFM (Eur. Financ. Manag.); ISSN 1354-7798 (print), 1468-036X (online)
- **Publisher**: Wiley-Blackwell (Wiley Online Library; hybrid, supports OA). Managed by the European Financial Management Association (EFMA). Submit via ScholarOne Manuscripts.
- **Tier**: Second-tier general finance (ABS/AJG 3, ABDC A, SSCI Q1/Q2). Strong European empirical/corporate finance journal — a realistic target for cross-asset volatility, risk management, or corporate-finance empirical work with an international/European angle.
- **IF 2024**: 4.42 | **5-yr IF**: ~3.2 (estimate) | **Quartile**: SSCI Q1/Q2
- **URL**: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/1468036x

## Scope
Research in all areas of financial management of modern corporations and financial institutions. Although European in name, the journal publishes international research with direct or indirect bearing on European markets, institutions, and policy. Special emphasis on empirical work relevant to practitioners and policymakers.
- **Fits**: empirical asset pricing with cross-country or European angle, volatility modeling with practical portfolio-management implication, corporate capital structure, dividend policy, mergers & acquisitions, market microstructure (European exchanges), risk management (VaR/ES panel across markets), hedge fund strategies, ESG/responsible finance.
- **Weak fit**: US-only studies with no international relevance, pure macro without finance application, pure methodology without empirical content (→ JoE/JBES), highly technical mathematical finance (→ QF).

## Format
- **Length**: no hard cap; typical papers 30-45 pages double-spaced. Word economy expected.
- **Abstract**: ≤100 words (hard cap per EFM author guidelines; confirmed via EFMA submission page).
- **Keywords/JEL**: up to 5 keywords; JEL classification code(s) required.
- **Structure**: IMRaD; double-anonymized (anonymized main file; separate title page). Authors are listed in alphabetical order by surname on the title page.
- **References**: author-date style (Harvard); DOIs where available.
- **Figures/tables**: editable; equations numbered; supplementary appendix for extra tables.
- **LaTeX / Word**: both accepted; Wiley provides LaTeX template at revision.

## Submission process
ScholarOne Manuscripts. **Submission fee USD 600** (non-refundable, mandatory before review). Required: anonymized manuscript, separate title page, abstract ≤100 w, up to 5 keywords + JEL code(s), data-availability statement, conflict-of-interest declaration, cover letter. ORCID encouraged. EFMA members may qualify for discount (verify at submission).

## Review model
Double-anonymized. Editor screens → 1-3 referees → decision. EFM is known for fast turnaround: "editorial decisions reached within four weeks from the submission date" (EFMA author guidelines). This is among the fastest in finance for a first decision. Acceptance rate ~15-20% (unverified estimate based on comparable field journals).

## Distinctive requirements
1. **Submission fee USD 600** (non-refundable, mandatory before review; EFMA member discount may apply). Higher fee than JBF ($350) or JoE ($75) — factor into expected-value calculation.
2. **Fast-track turnaround is a key feature**: EFM commits to 4-week first decision. Given the $600 fee, fast feedback reduces the opportunity cost of a desk-reject.
3. **Abstract ≤100 words** — tighter than most finance journals (JBF ≤150; JEmpFin ≤150); count carefully.
4. **Alphabetical author order** on title page (EFMA policy).
5. **Data-availability statement** required (Wiley/EFMA policy).
6. **European/international angle expected** — pure US-only studies without European context often desk-rejected.
7. **Double-blind review**: fully anonymize manuscript (names, affiliations, self-cites 3rd person, acknowledgements stripped).
8. Generative-AI declaration section required if AI used in preparation (Wiley 2024 policy).

## Pre-submission checklist (extends generic)
- Abstract ≤100 words (count carefully), unstructured, no refs; up to 5 keywords + JEL.
- Manuscript fully anonymized; separate title page with all author info + alphabetical surname order.
- Verify international/European relevance is explicit in intro (why does this matter for non-US markets?).
- Data-availability statement included; code/data package prepared.
- COI and funding declarations completed.
- Editable source (.tex Wiley template or .docx); no PDF as submission.
- Generative-AI declaration section if applicable.
- Econometric rigor: correct OOS design, lagged signals, DM/HLN/SPA + HAC, QLIKE = actual/predicted, cluster-robust or date-aggregated inference.
- Multiple-testing control (Reality Check/SPA/Romano-Wolf/MCS) for comparative claims.
- Robustness: multiple periods incl. crisis/bear, alt specs, subsample stability.
- Position vs recent EFM + comparable journals (JBF, JEmpFin, PBFJ); 2-3 concrete contributions.
- Harvard author-date refs + DOIs; verify in-text ↔ list matches.
