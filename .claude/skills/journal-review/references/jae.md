# Journal of Applied Econometrics (JAE)

- **Abbrev / ISSN**: JAE (J. Appl. Econ.); ISSN 0883-7252 (print), 1099-1255 (online)
- **Publisher**: John Wiley & Sons (Wiley Online Library; hybrid, supports OA). Submit via Editorial Express portal.
- **Tier**: Top-field applied econometrics (ABS/AJG 4, ABDC A*, SSCI Q1). Together with JBES, the flagship venue for empirical/applied econometric work that combines new methodology with economically substantive application.

## Scope
Empirical/applied econometric papers with a strong quantitative/statistical component and clear economic substance. Special issues on high-frequency, panel, forecasting, machine-learning-in-econometrics, financial econometrics. Software/data replication is a core editorial value (JAE Data Archive since 1994 = the discipline's earliest replication mandate).
- **Fits**: applied time-series (GARCH/HAR/regime-switching/state-space), forecasting evaluation (DM/MCS/SPA), panel dynamics, financial-econometrics case studies where the estimator or evaluation framework is the point.
- **Weak fit**: pure theoretical econometrics with no application (→ JoE), pure finance with off-the-shelf methods (→ JBF/JEmpFin), single-country descriptive replications, opinion/practitioner pieces.

## Format
- **Length**: soft ceiling ~40 double-spaced pages incl. tables/figures/refs (main text usually 25-35 pp); no hard cap.
- **Abstract**: unstructured, ≤150 words typical (no strict count in guide; keep ≤150 to match sister journals).
- **Keywords/JEL**: 3-5 keywords + JEL codes required.
- **Structure**: IMRaD; free-format submission (Wiley policy): initial submission may follow author's own formatting; formal Wiley/JAE style only required at revision.
- **References**: author-date Harvard style; DOIs where available.
- **Figures/tables**: editable; equations numbered; supplementary appendix acceptable and encouraged for detailed derivations / additional tables.
- **LaTeX / Word**: both accepted; Wiley provides LaTeX template at revision stage.

## Submission process
Editorial Express (`https://editorialexpress.com/`). **No submission fee**. Required at initial submission: manuscript (PDF acceptable at first pass under free-format), abstract, JEL codes, keywords, cover letter (short, describing contribution). Editorial policy caps concurrent submissions: no author may have >3 papers under review at JAE simultaneously.

## Review model
Double-anonymized. Editor assessment → 1-2 referees → decision. First decision typically ~3-4 months (estimate based on published Editor's Reports; unverified for 2025). High desk-reject rate for scope misfit (methodology-thin applications, theoretical papers with no data).

## Fees & OA
No submission fee. Hybrid: subscription route free; gold OA APC USD 3,720 (Wiley 2025 rate, verify live). Wiley transformative agreements (Read & Publish) waive APC for many institutions.

## Distinctive requirements
1. **JAE Data Archive** — MANDATORY on acceptance: authors must deposit data + code (Fortran/GAUSS/Matlab/R/Python/Stata) that replicate every table and figure in the paper. This is the OLDEST replication mandate in econometrics (since 1994) and is enforced pre-publication.
2. **Free-format initial submission** (Wiley): first-round formatting relaxed; strict style at revision.
3. **≤3 concurrent submissions per author**.
4. **Application-first framing**: empirical case must be economically motivated, not just a methodology showcase.
5. **Rejected papers cannot be resubmitted** to JAE unless invited by editor.
6. **ORCID recommended** at submission, required at acceptance (Wiley policy).

## Pre-submission checklist (extends generic)
- Abstract ≤150 words, unstructured, no refs (count it).
- 3-5 keywords + JEL codes on title page.
- Free-format initial PDF OK; save Wiley LaTeX conversion for revision.
- **Data + code package ready** for Data Archive upload on acceptance (test from clean clone; every table/figure reproducible; README + fixed seeds + pinned environment). Not required at submission but reviewers will flag if the package looks unlikely to satisfy the archive gate.
- Verify no >3 other JAE submissions in flight from the author.
- Fit/framing: lead with an ECONOMIC/FINANCIAL question that motivates the econometric choice, not with the estimator itself.
- Econometric rigor: proper OOS design, lagged signals (`signal.shift(1)`), DM/HLN/SPA with small-sample correction, QLIKE = actual/predicted, cluster-robust or date-aggregated inference (no asset-day iid pooling).
- Multiple-testing/data-snooping control (Reality Check/SPA/Romano-Wolf/MCS); explicit statement of null and alternative.
- Robustness: multiple periods incl. crisis/bear subsample, alternative specifications, sensitivity to hyperparameters (dedicated section expected).
- Cite ≥2 recent JAE papers to position; contribution stated in 2-3 concrete bullets.
- Author-date Harvard refs + DOIs; all in-text ↔ list matches; supplementary appendix for long derivations.
