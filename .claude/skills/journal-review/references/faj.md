# Financial Analysts Journal (FAJ)

- **Abbrev / ISSN**: FAJ; ISSN 0015-198X (print), 1938-3312 (online). Quarterly + online ahead-of-print.
- **Publisher**: Routledge / Taylor & Francis for CFA Institute (FAJ is its flagship, since 1945).
- **Tier**: Top practitioner / field-flagship in investment management. Q1 Finance (SJR), CiteScore ~4.1. Prestigious, highly selective practitioner-facing. Best fit when VolPred work has clear, implementable investment-practice payoff after costs.

## Scope
Advance the PRACTICE of investment management via rigorous, peer-reviewed, practitioner-relevant research + opinion pieces.
- **Fits**: findings useful for practitioners with application beyond a particular region/sector; majority in portfolio management + wealth planning. Must incorporate practical considerations (implementation cost of trading rules; only information available at design time).
- **Does NOT fit**: excessively theoretical/technical; narrow single-region/sector w/o broad practical implications; trading rules w/o implementation-cost analysis; strategies using info not available at design time; large overlap w/ other work (redundant publication); promotional/critical pieces about specific products/firms/individuals.

## Format
- **Length**: research articles "somewhat shorter than typical academic articles" — guideline says 10-20k words but write concisely (well under ~10k in practice). Perspectives: 3,000-5,000 w, little/no math, few refs.
- **Abstract**: unstructured, ONE paragraph **~100 words**; simple language, NO technical terms, NO refs/citations.
- **Keywords/JEL**: free-typed keywords (≥1 keyword + ≥2 classifications in portal); seed keywords in title/abstract/intro. JEL NOT used.
- **Structure**: Intro has NO heading and NO road-map/section preview — opens with first paragraph; must state+motivate the question, highlight practitioner relevance, connect to literature, + ≥1 distinct paragraph on findings + practical applications in simple language. Conclusion highlights major results + practitioner takeaways. Tables/figures at END; robustness tables → appendix. Two tracks: Research Articles + Perspectives (both double-blind).
- **References**: author/date (Chicago + CFA Institute Style Guide); footnotes minimized; FAJ-style journal citations.
- **Figures/tables**: minimal; tables ≤8 columns, portrait; ALL must be legible in BLACK & WHITE; at end of manuscript. Significance via * (5% two-tail) / ** (1%); when asterisks used, do NOT also report p-values.
- **LaTeX**: NO template / not preferred. Microsoft WORD, 12pt serif (Times New Roman/Bookman), double-spaced, single-column, equations in MathType (convert to PDF for best results).

## Submission process
Taylor & Francis Submission Portal (Chrome-optimized; existing Editorial Manager/T&F Online account does NOT carry over). Submitting author must be an actual author → corresponding author. Prepare TWO versions: full (w/ author info) + FULLY ANONYMIZED (no names/affiliations/emails/identifying info incl. Word metadata) for double-blind. Filenames alphanumeric + underscores only. Provide ≥1 keyword, ≥2 classifications, affiliation for ALL coauthors, ORCID (encouraged), billing address + payment method (Visa/MC/Amex) at Checkout before final submit. **MANDATORY one-page cover letter** (describe manuscript + why it fits FAJ; for research articles highlight question/results/takeaways/practical relevance — pasting the abstract is NOT sufficient, non-conforming letters returned). Resubmissions: point-by-point response in cover letter. Declarations: original/unpublished/not under review; full COI per coauthor in portal; permissions; read FAJ/CFA policies. No formal suggested-reviewers field.

## Review model
DOUBLE-BLIND for Research Articles + Perspectives. Editorial desk screen (non-fitting/unlikely-to-survive returned w/o external review) → strip identifiers → typically TWO external reviewers. Crossref Similarity Check on all submissions + again before publication. R&R common; authors always get feedback + reports. ~64 d avg to first decision. Highly selective (exact rate not public).

## Fees & OA
Submission fee UNCERTAIN/changing: 2017 guidelines say "no submission fee at this time", but the current portal includes a mandatory billing + Checkout + payment-method step before final submit (implies a fee now exists) — exact amount NOT publicly documented; the "$50" online refers to a Joint World Bank-FAJ CONFERENCE submission, not the journal. VERIFY in the live portal. Hybrid OA (T&F Open Select); optional gold-OA APC (not confirmed; check T&F Open Access Cost Finder; read-and-publish deals may waive). Separately: rolling 12-month OA — every article free for one year from publication regardless of OA election (older archives subscriber-only + JSTOR back to 1960).

## Distinctive requirements
1. PRACTITIONER RELEVANCE = top acceptance criterion (practical value, implementation costs, only design-time information); purely theoretical/technical rejected. 2. ~100-word, single-paragraph, jargon-free abstract, NO citations. 3. Intro has NO heading + NO roadmap. 4. Title concise, preferably ≤80 chars incl. spaces. 5. Strict double-blind anonymization INCLUDING Word metadata; self-cite in third person. 6. Tables ≤8 cols, portrait, B&W-legible, at end. 7. Significance asterisks (* 5%, ** 1%), NO p-values when asterisks used. 8. Mandatory one-page cover letter (more than a pasted abstract). 9. Replication expectation — be ready to share code/programs (and permitted data) if findings questioned. 10. Written specifically for FAJ practitioner readership, no large overlap w/ other work. No highlights / graphical abstract.

## Pre-submission checklist (extends generic)
- State the practitioner payoff in the FIRST paragraph — what investment decision improves + by how much AFTER realistic transaction/implementation costs. FAJ rejects look-ahead or cost-ignoring backtests (verify signal.shift(1) lag + cost assumptions for vol-targeting work).
- Confirm the result generalizes beyond a single region/sector/asset; a TAIFEX-only study needs an explicit argument for a global-practitioner audience.
- Keep exposition approachable to a CFA charterholder/generalist; heavy derivations → appendix; body practitioner-facing, not econometrics-heavy.
- Abstract ONE paragraph ~100 w, plain language, zero citations, zero technical notation.
- Intro NO heading + NO roadmap; motivates question + ties to literature + ≥1 paragraph on findings + practical implications in simple language.
- Title ≤80 chars incl. spaces; key keywords in title/abstract/intro for SEO.
- Author/date Chicago + CFA Institute style refs, alphabetical; footnotes minimized.
- Tables ≤8 cols, portrait, B&W-legible, at END; figures B&W-safe.
- Significance via * (5%) / ** (1%); remove p-values where asterisks used.
- MS Word, 12pt serif, double-spaced, single column, MathType equations (do NOT submit LaTeX/PDF source).
- Two files: author version + fully anonymized version (scrub Word metadata; third-person self-cites); filenames alphanumeric + underscores.
- One-page cover letter (fit + question + key results + practical relevance; NOT just the abstract); coauthor affiliations + ≥1 keyword + ≥2 classifications ready; original/not-under-review + COI confirmed; replication code/data ready to share; verify whether the live portal now charges a fee before checkout.
