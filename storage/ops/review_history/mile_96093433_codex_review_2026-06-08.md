## Findings

1. **The article overstates the second leg of “Trinity”: the source does not implement full Christoffersen conditional coverage.**  
   The article repeatedly frames the formal gate as “Kupiec + 條件覆蓋（CC） + Basel” and calls this “Trinity” in [storage/reports/feed.json](/Users/yhlai0911/Desktop/volpred-research/storage/reports/feed.json:62). But `christoffersen_test()` in [experiments/k893/k893_regime_conformal_var.py](/Users/yhlai0911/Desktop/volpred-research/experiments/k893/k893_regime_conformal_var.py:303) only computes the **independence** likelihood-ratio test (`lr_ind`) from hit transitions. It does **not** compute the full conditional-coverage LR that combines independence and unconditional coverage. Then `trinity_test()` simply combines `kupiec_test + christoffersen_test + basel_traffic_light`; see [experiments/k893/k893_regime_conformal_var.py](/Users/yhlai0911/Desktop/volpred-research/experiments/k893/k893_regime_conformal_var.py:383). So the published article is claiming a stronger backtesting battery than the code actually runs.

2. **The article presents “Conformal VaR” as if the implementation carries standard conformal-test semantics, but the code is a weighted residual quantile engine without a recognizable conformal calibration step.**  
   In the source, `compute_rwc_var()` just takes past standardized residuals, applies exponential VIX-distance weights, and returns a weighted empirical quantile; see [experiments/k893/k893_regime_conformal_var.py](/Users/yhlai0911/Desktop/volpred-research/experiments/k893/k893_regime_conformal_var.py:226). There is no explicit split-conformal / calibration-set construction, no nonconformity-score update rule beyond standardized residuals, and no finite-sample conformal guarantee derivation in the artifact. Calling the method “regime-weighted conformal VaR” may be an internal naming convention, but the article states it more strongly than the implementation justifies.

3. **Several mechanism claims are inferences, not source-verified findings, and should be labeled as such.**  
   The article explains λ=0.2 failure through “effective sample 縮減到某個臨界值以下” and says λ=0.05 works because it conditions mainly in the Medium regime while avoiding the dangerous effective-sample zone in [storage/reports/feed.json](/Users/yhlai0911/Desktop/volpred-research/storage/reports/feed.json:62). But `K893` never computes effective sample size, never reports such a threshold, and does not run a diagnostic tying failures to ESS. Those statements are plausible interpretations, but they are not empirically established by the artifact and should not be written as if directly measured.

## What Holds

1. **Most headline numbers in the article do match the experiment artifact.**  
   The 99% VaR numbers quoted for SPY, QQQ, and 0050.TW align with [experiments/k893/k893_regime_conformal_var_results.json](/Users/yhlai0911/Desktop/volpred-research/experiments/k893/k893_regime_conformal_var_results.json:1). For example:
   - SPY `HistSim` avg VaR `% = -3.2510`, `RWC λ=0.05 = -3.1124`, implying about `4.3%` tighter.
   - QQQ `Student-t` Kupiec `p = 0.0002`, `RWC λ=0.05` violation rate `1.04%`, Kupiec `p = 0.8571`.
   - 0050.TW `RWC λ=0.2` Kupiec `p = 0.0410`, which is indeed a fail at 5%.

2. **No obvious lookahead bug is present in the regime weighting.**  
   The OOS loop explicitly uses `vix_vals[t - 1]` as the regime input for day `t`, documented as “NO LOOKAHEAD”; see [experiments/k893/k893_regime_conformal_var.py](/Users/yhlai0911/Desktop/volpred-research/experiments/k893/k893_regime_conformal_var.py:563).

3. **The practical message “λ=0.05 is a modest refinement, λ=0.2 is fragile” is directionally supported.**  
   The code/results do support that `RWC_lam0.05` often gives a slightly less negative average VaR than `HistSim` while preserving the source’s own `trinity_pass` flag at 99%, whereas `RWC_lam0.2` fails on SPY and 0050.TW and also fails the ES test on QQQ.

## Conclusion

This article is **not a fabricated-stats failure**, but it does fail the 24h source-integrity standard because the published statistical framing is stronger than the implementation:

- `CC` is described as Christoffersen conditional coverage, while the code only runs the independence component;
- `Trinity` is therefore overstated as a stricter gate than what was actually executed;
- `Conformal VaR` and `effective sample threshold` language read more theoretically established than the artifact supports.

To recover the piece, the article should be narrowed to wording closer to:

- “Kupiec + hit-independence + Basel GREEN”
- “VIX-weighted standardized-residual quantile VaR”
- “effective-sample explanation is a plausible mechanism, not a directly estimated result”
