But the published narrative has multiple material source mismatches or unsupported macro claims: FOMC sample wording is wrong, one relative MOVE comparison is false, one chart interpretation is false, VIX percentile is unsupported/mismatched, and the dot-plot/SOFR/skew claims are not generated by the experiment.

## Findings

1. **False relative comparison: article line 43 says current MOVE 69.4 is below all sampled FOMC T-1 levels except 2026-01-29.**
   - Source values: 77.1, **67.3**, 75.4, **57.4**, 81.3, 74.3 (`results.json:21-115`).
   - Current 69.4 is above both 2025-10-29 (67.3) and 2026-01-29 (57.4).
   - Correct wording: "低於六場中的四場，高於 2025-10-29 與 2026-01-29。"

2. **False sample wording: article line 25 says "2025 年以來全部六場 FOMC".**
   - Source only hardcodes `2025-09-17` to `2026-04-30` (`fetch_data.py:22-30`), i.e. "最近六場 / 自 2025-09 以來六場".
   - It is not "all FOMC since 2025".

3. **Unsupported central macro claims: dot-plot dispersion and SOFR futures are not in source/results.**
   - Article lines 13, 15, 67, 89 claim:
     - dot-plot dispersion is the largest this year
     - SR3Z26 implied rate is about 3.96%
     - SR3U26 needs to move from 3.81% toward 3.55%
   - Experiment only fetches yfinance `^MOVE`, `^VIX`, `^VIX9D`, `TLT`, `SHV`, `^IRX`, `SPY` (`fetch_data.py:33-42`).
   - No SOFR futures, no Fed SEP/dot-plot data, no dispersion metric, and no source citation in `results.json`.
   - This is material because the headline and framing depend on "dot-plot divergence" plus "rates options underpricing".

4. **VIX percentile claim is unsupported and numerically off.**
   - Article line 89 says VIX is about P58 using 6/12 level.
   - `results.json:7-12` has 6/15 VIX as `NaN`, VIX percentile as 0.0, and VIX9D/VIX as `NaN`; source does not record the 6/12 fallback rank.
   - Recomputing from `raw_close.csv` with 6/12 VIX 17.68 gives about **P55.3**, not P58.

5. **Chart interpretation mismatch: article line 81 says one-year MOVE peaked in March at about 85 around 3/19 FOMC eve.**
   - `raw_close.csv` shows one-year MOVE max **115.02 on 2026-03-26**.
   - 3/18 T-1 MOVE was 81.25 (`results.json:85-89`), but it was not the one-year peak.
   - Correct wording: "3/19 FOMC 前夕 MOVE 約 81；同月 3/26 一度升到約 115。"

6. **TLT option skew / cheap tail insurance claim is not measured.**
   - Article line 69 discusses TLT option skew and protective-put premium behavior.
   - Source fetches TLT price only and computes realized volatility; no option chain, skew, put premium, or implied-vs-realized spread is present.
   - The statement can remain as market intuition only if clearly labeled as mechanism/speculation, not as a result from this experiment.

7. **Small-n overframing risk.**
   - The "past six FOMC" SPY -0.96% / 2-of-6 result is descriptive only (`n=6`) and no statistical test is run.
   - Article mostly avoids formal significance claims, but lines 47 and 93 should not be extended into a predictive claim about the 2026-06-17 event.

8. **Figure rendering issue.**
   - Local rendered figures show CJK tofu/square glyphs in titles/legends because `make_figure.py:58` forces `DejaVu Sans`, which lacks full Chinese coverage.
   - This does not change numeric claims but affects published chart quality.
